Wake test

?Wiring the meter (arm first, with the lamp wired as normal):
  1. Cut the power. Unplug USB too (on USB the meter reads nothing). Candle out.
  2. Move the FireBeetle's VCC wire from the BAT row to an empty row (row X).
  3. Red probe in the 10A jack, dial on 10A, black probe in COM.
  4. Red probe on a jumper from the BAT row (pack +), black probe on a jumper from row X.
  5. Power back on. The lamp boots, syncs, takes the test and sleeps; the countdown starts. Ignore the meter until 0.
If the lamp is already running on the meter, hold NEXT 5 s to make it check in. The TEG monitor connected over USB holds the lamp awake. connecting…

iSyncing: the normal 10-minute wake (WiFi on, check the worker, back to sleep).
Bare: boots and goes straight back to sleep, no WiFi. Its reading is the local current, which splits a syncing wake into local + WiFi.
Button press: your next real NEXT press from sleep is the measured wake (no countdown). Step through as many images as you like; it sleeps 10 s after your last press. Logs image steps and full vs partial refreshes.
Sleep: add by hand in the log (mA jack).
