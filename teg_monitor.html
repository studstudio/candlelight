model

Pack gauge: counts LTC4150 charge in and out from the last full. Full is marked by hand, or automatically when the candle is lit, charge was flowing, and then no charge pulse comes for 20 min (the BQ25504 has stopped at its limit). Charge is an estimate: V²/(4R) at the TEG's best point, times booster efficiency, over pack voltage. Break-even counts down what this session has already harvested against a day's use, at the current rate. Warning above 2.8 V (BQ25504 input limit 3 V).

The lamp stays awake with WiFi on (~100 mA) and reports its LTC4150 count with a sync every 2 min; the worker adds up what left the pack (out − in) and switches drain off at the target, then the lamp sleeps as usual. Wiring: pack + → LTC IN, LTC OUT row → BQ BAT and FireBeetle VCC (not its BAT/JST socket), GND common, USB unplugged (on USB nothing drains). The lamp picks up a start at its next scheduled sync (up to 10 min while it sleeps), or at once with a 5 s NEXT hold; "Draining" shows top-left while it runs. It stops by itself after an hour without reaching the worker. Afterwards let the pack rest 10 min before reading its voltage.
