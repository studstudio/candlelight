2 · wire the meter in, then power on
  1. Cut the power. Unplug USB from the FireBeetle too (on USB the meter reads nothing). Candle out.
  2. Take the wire to the FireBeetle's VCC pin out of the BAT row and put it in an empty row (row X).
  3. Red probe in the 10A jack, dial on 10A. Black probe in COM.
  4. Hold the red probe on a jumper from the BAT row (pack +), the black probe on a jumper from row X.
  5. Power back on. The lamp boots, syncs, takes the test and goes to sleep. The countdown below starts then. Ignore the meter until it reaches 0.

A line for every wake: what woke it, how long it was up, WiFi and sync time, new images, button presses, screen refreshes, signal. The lamp sends these with its next sync, so a wake shows up one sync later (button wakes wait for the next 10-minute sync). The dim "→ sync" lines appear live, as each sync reaches the worker. The lines are kept in the lamp's flash until sent, so a power cut doesn't lose them. A time with ~ is approximate: that wake came after a power cut and before the lamp could sync its clock.

Every measured wake lands here by itself with its type, firmware and timings. The lamp sends them at its next sync, normally the power-up after you wire it back. Type the meter's steadiest reading into its row, in mA (0.09 on the 10A dial = 90). Pack volts and a note are optional. Each field saves when you leave it. The log is kept on the worker, not in this browser.
